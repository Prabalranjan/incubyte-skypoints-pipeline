Calculates completed years. Member only turns a year older on or after their birthday anniversary in the target year. Handles leap-day (Feb 29) births safely.

⏱️ Stale Member Rule (Strict > 90 Days Boundary)

Assessment requirement: "Stale means days since Flight_Date greater than 90." Exact 90 days = ACTIVE; 91 days = STALE.

Click "Check Staleness" to test the exact boundary logic.
🌍 Country Relocation (Cross-Table Deactivation)

Scenario: Member relocates from India to Australia across consecutive day files.

• Day 1 (2024-01-10): Registered in India
→ Table_India: is_active = 'A'
• Day 2 (2024-01-15): Relocates to Australia
→ Table_Australia: is_active = 'A' (INSERTED)
→ Table_India: is_active = 'I' (DEACTIVATED)
Guarantees: Exactly 1 active profile across all tables in Current_Member_Profiles!
🛡️ Late-Arriving File Protection

Scenario: Out-of-order deliveries. Day 1 file arrives AFTER Day 2 file.

• Day 2 arrives first (2024-01-15):
→ Profile updated to Platinum ('PLT')
• Day 1 arrives late (2024-01-10):
→ PROTECTED & SKIPPED by CountryRouter
→ Reason: timestamp (2024-01-10) < existing (2024-01-15)
Guarantees: Current profile does NOT regress to older Silver ('SLV') state!
